    Pricing, plainly.

    Is there a first-year discount?

    Yes. Your first year costs less: 19% off every licence on monthly billing and 18% on annual billing. From year 2 you pay the standard prices shown above. Server upgrades and services are not discounted.

    Who counts as a full user?

    Anyone who creates invoices, posts entries, moves stock or works in the system every day. People who only need their own payslips, leave and expenses can be team members, and everyone else on payroll is free.

    How do you compare with Odoo, Zoho One and Daftra?

    See the side-by-side pages: Prospera vs Odoo, Prospera vs Zoho One and Prospera vs Daftra. Each one prices your company both ways, with every fee shown.

    What is the small-team cap?

    For small companies where most people log in, your user total never goes above EGP 1,527 a month for each employee on payroll (EGP 1,256 on annual billing). It keeps small companies, where most people log in, on a low bill.

    What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

    Annual billing is paid yearly and costs less: EGP 2,760 instead of 3,457 for each of the first two users, and EGP 680 instead of 857 for every user after that.

    What is Prospera Care?

    Every plan includes hours with Prospera experts every month: 1 hour for 1 to 3 full users, 2 hours for 4 to 10, 3 hours for 11 to 25, and 5 hours for 26 or more. Use them for what you need that month: closing the books with an accountant, training a new hire, a report, workflow or dashboard built with standard tools, or a process review. Hours start after go-live, are booked in advance and held remotely during support hours (Sunday to Thursday, 8:00 to 18:00 Cairo time), and do not carry over. Custom code, data migration, new modules and integrations are quoted separately.

    Is hosting included?

    Yes. Every client gets their own Basic server: 3 CPU cores, 4 GB RAM, 100 GB SSD and 100 GB of backup, not a shared one. As you grow, move up to Professional (4 cores, 8 GB, 160 GB SSD and backup) for EGP 6,540 a month, or Advanced (6 cores, 16 GB, 240 GB SSD and backup) for EGP 10,630 a month. The first-year discount applies to licences, not to server upgrades.

    Is setup included?

    No. Setup and training are priced for a fixed scope after a free systems review, so you know the full cost before you start.

    Are there limits on invoices, clients or branches?

    No. Invoices, clients, warehouses, branches and companies never change the bill. Only people do.

    Do you have prices for the Gulf and other countries?

    Yes, on request. The prices on this page are for companies in Egypt billed in Egyptian pounds.
