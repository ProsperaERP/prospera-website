    What "keeping it right" means

    Seven promises, every month.

    Not a slogan. This is the work we do for you, written down, so you can hold us to it.

    01

    Compliance kept current

    When the tax authority, VAT rules, social insurance or payroll law change, we update your system's tax and payroll settings before rule changes cost you, and flag any e-invoice ETA or ZATCA rejects so your team can correct it.

    02

    A health check every month

    We review your books, stock and banks every month for the problems that quietly grow: unposted entries, negative stock, unreconciled banks, duplicate customers. We flag what needs attention and show your team exactly how to fix it. Problems in the system's setup, we fix ourselves.

    03

    Month-end, reviewed with you

    We give your accountant a close checklist, review the results and flag anything that looks wrong before the owner sees it. The owner gets a monthly pack: profit, cash, receivables and stock, in plain numbers.

    04

    Changes as you grow

    A new branch, warehouse, price list, approval or user is set up by us inside your support hours. No new project, no new quote.

    05

    Updates, tested first

    New versions are tried on a copy of your system before they reach you. Hosting, backups, two-step sign-in and security are ours to handle.

    06

    People change, knowledge stays

    When an accountant or storekeeper leaves, we train the replacement. Your setup is documented, so nothing walks out of the door.

    07

    One team to call

    No vendor blaming the implementer blaming your IT. One named team, in Arabic or English, accountable for all of it.
