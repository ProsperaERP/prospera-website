Layer 01 · The core

The books and the operations, together.

Prospera ERP is the base of everything. Every sale, stock movement and payslip posts to the same set of books the moment it happens.

Accounting

Chart of accounts, automatic journals, banks, treasuries, cheques, cost centers and fixed assets.

Stock

Warehouses, batch, serial and expiry tracking, transfers, landed cost and pick lists.

Sales and point of sale

Quotes, orders, invoices, price lists, offers and point of sale, with e-invoicing on the same record.

Purchasing

Requests, supplier quotes, purchase orders and bills, matched to what was received.

HR and payroll

Employees, attendance, leave and payroll, posted to the accounts by cost center.

Projects

Projects, job costing, milestone billing and timesheets.
